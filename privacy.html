1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is A-Z immigration services s.r.o., IČO 17166721, Gercenova 900/11, 102 00 Praha, Česko. Contact for all privacy questions and requests: info@azis.cz.

2. What we collect

Account: your e-mail address, or the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google, your app language and notification settings.

Your case and profile details stay on your phone: case number and visa application number, dates, status and reminder settings, nationality, purpose of stay, permit type, city, OAMP office, and your name as in your passport (only if you turn on the missing-document search by name). We never save them on our server.

Device: a push notification token, so we can send you notifications. Case reminders are set up by the app on your phone.

Purchases: your plan and credit balance. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card.

Feedback you send us.

Chats with Karel AI: see section 4.

3. Why we use it

To provide the app you signed up for (contract): your account, your chat history, Karel AI answers, case tracking, reminders and missing-document checks.

With your consent: personalized offers based on your chats (section 4) and the missing-document search by passport name. When you accept these terms you also give your explicit consent to storing health information you write in your chats (section 4). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what happened before.

Our legitimate interest: keeping the service secure, preventing abuse (for example usage limits) and fixing errors.

4. Chats with Karel AI

Your chats with Karel are saved on our server (EU, Ireland) as your chat history, linked to your account, so you see them on any phone where you sign in. Only you can read them. We save your questions as you write them and Karel’s answers, also when they are about health. Photos and PDFs you attach in Analyze are not saved. Chats are deleted after 12 months.

To answer, your phone sends your question, recent messages, any photo or PDF from Analyze, and the profile and case facts that help Karel (permit type, type of proceeding, city, nationality, visa expiry, case status; never your case number) to our server and to the AI provider that writes the answer (Anthropic or Groq, USA). We don’t save the profile and case facts. With your own AI key, your question goes to our server to find relevant facts and is saved in your history with the answer; the AI request itself goes from your phone straight to your provider.

Personalized offers (optional, off unless you turn it on): we use your chats about places, products and services (for example cafés, shops, flats or translators) to understand what users need and to show you relevant offers. Questions about health, doctors, medicines or symptoms are never used for offers.

You can turn personalized offers off at any time in Account. Deleting your account deletes your chats. To have chats deleted earlier, write to info@azis.cz.

5. Location

Your location is used only when you ask for something nearby. For map links it stays on your phone. For the doctor search, your approximate position or the address you type is sent to our server for that one search and is not stored. To find the doctors and the address, it is passed on to Golemio (Prague’s open-data platform), the Czech address register (ČÚZK) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

6. Official sources

The case status check opens the official website ipc.gov.cz on your phone; we save only the resulting status to your case. For missing documents, your phone sends your case number (and your passport name, if you turned this on) to our server for one check when you open the app. We compare it with our copy of the Ministry of the Interior’s public notices and send any matches back to your phone. We don’t save the number, the name or the matches.

7. Who processes data for us

Supabase (database and server, data stored in the EU, Ireland); Anthropic and Groq (AI answers, USA); Expo (push notifications, USA) with Apple and Google; Apple, Google and RevenueCat (sign-in and purchases, USA); Golemio, ČÚZK and the OpenStreetMap Foundation (UK) for the doctor search.

When data goes outside the EU, we rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your personal data.

8. How long we keep data

Account data: until you delete your account. Your cases, profile details, passport name and the notices found for you: only on your phone, until you delete them or the app. Chats with Karel: 12 months on our server, then deleted automatically. Usage-limit records: about one day. Push tokens: until you sign out or delete your account.

When you delete your account, your data on our server is deleted, including your chats. Copies in our providers’ backups disappear within their backup cycles. Feedback you sent us is kept, without the link to your account.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict its use, receive it in a portable format, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time.

Write to info@azis.cz. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (www.uoou.gov.cz).

10. Children

The app is not meant for children under 15, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Security

Your data is protected by access rules so that only you can see your own data, including your chats, and by encrypted connections. Your case and profile details, your passport name and the keys you add for your own AI stay on your phone.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app and, where needed, ask for your consent again.
